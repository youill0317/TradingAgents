PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Walmart Inc. is a people-led, technology-powered omni-channel retailer that helps customers save money and live better, through retail stores, clubs and eCommerce sites in the U.S. and abroad. Its fiscal year ends on January 31.

The Company operates three reportable segments: Walmart U.S., Walmart International and Sam’s Club U.S.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The risks described below could materially and adversely affect the Company’s business, results of operations, financial condition and liquidity.

Strategic risks

The Company faces strong competition from other retailers, wholesale club operators and omni-channel retailers, which could materially adversely affect its financial performance.

Financial risks

Fluctuations in foreign exchange rates may materially adversely affect the results of operations of the Walmart International segment.

None.
